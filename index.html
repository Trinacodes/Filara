HOW TO MEASURE

  • Measure around fullest part of bust, tape parallel to floor.
  • Measure waist at thinnest place.
  • Measure fullest part of hips.
  • For tops: measure from highest point of shoulder to desired length.
  • Measure from shoulder seam to wrist bone with arm slightly bent.

SIZE TIPS

  • If between sizes, size up for comfort.
  • Crochet has slight natural stretch, molds to body.
  • Handmade items may vary 1–2 cm from listed measurements.
  • For questions, contact us before ordering.
  • If you have a bigger/smaller bust or hips, add in order note (e.g. size S bust, M hips).

Some things begin quietly.

Filara was not born in a design studio or a fashion school. It was born in a moment of stillness — a young woman, a hook, and a thread, teaching herself something beautiful from scratch.

Today, Filara is a small atelier dedicated to handcrafted crochet that honors slowness, craftsmanship, and the beauty of imperfection. Each piece is made with intention, meant to be worn and cherished for years.

We believe luxury is not defined by price, but by the hands behind every stitch.
