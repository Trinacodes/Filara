HOW TO MEASURE

  • Measure around fullest part of bust, tape parallel to floor.
  • Measure waist at thinnest place.
  • Measure fullest part of hips.
  • For tops: measure from highest point of shoulder to desired length.
  • Measure from shoulder seam to wrist bone with arm slightly bent.

SIZE TIPS

  • If between sizes, size up for comfort.
  • Crochet has slight natural stretch, molds to body.
  • Handmade items may vary 1–2 cm from listed measurements.
  • For questions, contact us before ordering.
  • If you have a bigger/smaller bust or hips, add in order note (e.g. size S bust, M hips).
